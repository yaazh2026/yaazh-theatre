Therukoothu Training Workshop and Performance

Handing over Therukoothu, the pioneering traditional art form of Tamil theatre, to the younger generation through a 10-day intensive residential workshop and community public performances in Puducherry—offered completely free of cost for marginalized youth.

Director: Dr. G. Gobi
Instructors: A. Senthil & A. Govindaraj
Cohort: 16 Artists
