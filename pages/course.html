Acting & Training

The training programmes have focused not only on acting techniques but also on developing the body, voice, imagination, emotional expression, creativity and stage presence of participants. Through regular classes, weekend programmes, workshops and practical theatre activities, participants have been introduced to both traditional and contemporary approaches to theatre.

Who is this for?

  • Aspiring professional actors looking for serious training.
  • Theatre educators wishing to deepen their pedagogical craft.
  • Performance artists exploring body-based expression.

Syllabus

The Curriculum

01

The Body as Instrument

Physical conditioning, flexibility, and spatial awareness using classical and contemporary techniques.

02

Voice & Resonators

Breath control, projection, and emotional coloring of the voice for different stage settings.

03

The Inner Landscape

Exploring subtext, character motivation, and psychological realism through intense workshop sessions.

04

The Live Production

Culminating in a public performance where students apply all learned techniques on a professional stage.

05

Styles of Acting

Naturalism, physical theatre, folk and classical forms, and camera acting explored as a living range, not a single method. Building an adaptable performer, not a fixed style.

06

Camera & Audition Craft

Screen presence, audition technique, and performance preparation for camera work. Bridging stage training with on-camera demands.
